About Us

Building Confidence Through Advanced Medicine & Genuine Compassion

Every individual deserves to be beautiful, and to be the best version of oneself inside out.

The gateway to your elegance and confidence is right here. Welcome to Ara Aesthetics!

Ara Aesthetics is the canopy for all cosmetic treatments envisaging to create a positive change in the lives of people.

Ara Aesthetics center is one of the leading cosmetic centers known for offering world-class cosmetic surgery in Theni. Dr. Senthil Kumar is one of the best cosmetic surgeons in Theni and is known for his experience and expertise in offering a wide range of surgical and non-surgical cosmetic procedures.

Vision

To bring positive changes in at least one million people by helping them to feel the best version of themselves. Happy, confident and beautiful inside one's own skin is achievable and must be reached.

Mission

To provide the highest possible quality of services with integrity and accountability in the field of aesthetic medicine and surgery. Respecting individual rights and understanding their concerns are way ward for Ara Aesthetics.

Our Founder

Dr.C.Senthil Kumar

M.S, MRCS (UK).,MCH

At Ara Aesthetics, we offer world-class aesthetic solutions which include surgical cosmetic procedures, non-surgical cosmetic procedures, hair care cosmetic procedures, cosmetic gynaecology and wellness.

Our visionary Dr. C. Senthil Kumar, with profound expertise in Cosmetology, is instrumental in the establishment and functioning of the center. He is a university gold medallist of the Tamilnadu Dr.MGR Medical University for plastic surgery and got his fellowship from the United Kingdom adding to his credit. He has practised in Birmingham and other parts of India. His prowess and work for more than a decade has created innumerable happiness, inspiring times of conviction and success.
