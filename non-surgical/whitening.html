Skin Whitening Treatment

Advanced clinical solutions to brighten your complexion and achieve an even, radiant skin tone.

Brighten and Glow

Reveal your brightest skin yet. Our Whitening treatments at Ara Aesthetics use safe and effective clinical protocols to reduce hyperpigmentation, even out skin tone, and enhance your natural radiance, giving you a luminous and glowing complexion.

  • What is it?
  • Benefits
  • FAQ
  • Gallery

Skin Whitening, often referred to as skin brightening or lightening, is a cosmetic procedure aimed at reducing the concentration of melanin in the skin. This helps in correcting uneven skin tones, fading dark spots, and revitalizing a tired-looking complexion.

Our approach combines clinical-grade active ingredients with advanced delivery systems to ensure that the brightening agents reach the deeper layers of your skin, providing long-lasting and noticeable improvements in your overall skin health and appearance.

Our Brightening Strategy

We use a multi-faceted approach to ensure safety and effectiveness:

  • Skin Assessment: Evaluating your skin type and the cause of pigmentation (sun damage, hormonal, or post-acne).
  • Professional Exfoliation: Removing dead skin cells to allow better penetration of brightening agents.
  • Serum Infusion: Using high-potency Vitamin C, Glutathione, and other antioxidants.
  • Protective Barriers: Applying nourishing masks that lock in the brightening ingredients.
  • Post-Care Guidance: Designing a home-care regimen to maintain and enhance the clinical results.

Benefits of Whitening Treatment

  • Significantly reduces hyperpigmentation and dark spots
  • Restores an even and balanced skin tone
  • Provides an immediate boost in skin radiance and luminosity
  • Deeply rejuvenates and improves skin texture
  • Helps in fading acne scars and age spots
  • Safe, non-invasive procedures with visible outcomes

FAQ

Is the treatment safe?

Yes, at Ara Aesthetics, we use only clinically-tested and dermatologist-approved ingredients. We avoid harsh chemicals and tailor the concentration of active agents to suit your skin's tolerance level.

How many sessions are recommended?

While an immediate glow is visible after the first session, we generally recommend a series of 4 to 6 sessions spaced 2 weeks apart for significant and lasting correction of pigmentation.

Will my skin become very sensitive?

Your skin may be slightly more sensitive to the sun immediately after treatment. This is why we emphasize the importance of daily broad-spectrum sunscreen application as part of your post-care routine.
