Leg D-Tan Treatment

Remove stubborn sun tan and restore the natural skin tone of your legs with our clinical D-Tan solutions.

Brighten Your Legs

Our legs are often exposed to the sun, leading to stubborn tanning and uneven skin tone. Our Leg D-Tan treatments at Ara Aesthetics use powerful yet safe clinical formulations to effectively break down tan pigment, leaving your legs looking brighter, smoother, and more even-toned.

  • What is it?
  • Benefits
  • FAQ
  • Gallery

Leg D-Tan is a targeted skin brightening procedure focusing on the unique needs of the skin on your legs, which is often thicker and more prone to stubborn tanning. Overexposure to the sun while wearing shorts or skirts can lead to significant pigment changes, especially around the knees and ankles.

Our treatment uses powerful yet safe de-tanning agents combined with intensive exfoliation to break down melanin buildup and reveal the smoother, lighter skin underneath. It's an essential treatment for anyone looking to maintain a uniform skin tone across their entire body.

The Leg D-Tan Process

A comprehensive approach to leg skin rejuvenation:

  • Surface Cleansing: Removing dirt and grime to allow active ingredients to work effectively.
  • Intensive Scrub: Using specialized scrubs to soften rough patches and remove dead skin.
  • Potent D-Tan Pack: Applying a high-concentration formula designed specifically for body skin.
  • Action Time: Allowing the pack to deeply penetrate and dissolve tan (20-25 minutes).
  • Deep Hydration: Finishing with a rich moisturizer to keep the legs feeling soft and supple.

Benefits of Leg D-Tan

  • Effectively removes stubborn sun tan and dark patches
  • Evens out skin tone from thighs to toes
  • Softens rough skin, especially around the knees and ankles
  • Deeply exfoliates and improves skin texture
  • Restores the natural radiance and glow of your legs
  • Perfect preparation for special occasions or beach trips

FAQ

Is it effective for dark knees?

Yes. We pay special attention to areas with thicker skin like the knees and ankles, where tanning and pigmentation tend to be more concentrated and stubborn.

How many sessions are needed for best results?

While you will see a visible difference in just one session, we recommend a series of 3 to 4 sessions for complete removal of deep-seated tanning.

Will it make my skin dry?

On the contrary, our treatment includes a deep hydration phase. However, we always recommend applying a good quality body lotion daily at home to maintain the results.
