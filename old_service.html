360° Liposuction

360 Degree Liposuction is an advanced body contouring procedure that comprehensively sculpts your entire midsection by addressing stubborn fat deposits from all angles. This revolutionary fat removal treatment targets the abdomen, flanks, love handles, and back in a single session, creating a naturally balanced and harmonious silhouette.

Unlike traditional liposuction that focuses on isolated areas, 360 Degree Liposuction provides complete circumferential contouring of your waistline. The procedure uses state-of-the-art technology including power-assisted liposuction (PAL) and ultrasound-assisted techniques to achieve precise body sculpting with minimal invasiveness.

At Ara Aesthetics, we have performed over 3,500 successful cosmetic surgeries, making us one of the most trusted centers for 360 Degree Liposuction in Theni. Our approach ensures natural-looking results that enhance your body's existing proportions while maintaining safety as our top priority.

Who can get 360 Degree Liposuction?

Ideal candidates include:

  • Adults with BMI under 30 who are close to their ideal weight
  • People with good skin elasticity and muscle tone
  • Individuals with stubborn fat deposits resistant to diet and exercise
  • Those seeking comprehensive midsection contouring
  • People with realistic expectations about surgical outcomes

Who cannot get 360 Degree Liposuction?

  • Individuals with excessive loose skin requiring surgical removal
  • People with bleeding disorders or clotting issues
  • Chain smokers (must quit at least 2 weeks prior)
  • Those with unrealistic expectations about results

Areas treated with 360 Degree Liposuction

360 Degree Liposuction comprehensively addresses multiple areas in a single procedure:

  • Front abdomen - Upper and lower belly fat
  • Flanks - Love handles and side rolls
  • Back area - Bra rolls and lower back fat
  • Waistline - Complete circumferential contouring
  • Hip area - Creating smooth transitions

The "360" approach ensures seamless contouring without visible demarcation lines, creating a naturally sculpted appearance that looks athletic and proportioned.

Why choose 360 Degree Liposuction?

Getting treatment from the best cosmetic surgery center offers numerous advantages:

  • Complete transformation - Address multiple problem areas simultaneously
  • Natural results - Seamless contouring without obvious surgical lines
  • Enhanced proportions - Better overall body balance and silhouette
  • Confidence boost - Achieve the body contours you've always desired
  • Clothing fit - Enjoy better-fitting clothes and increased wardrobe options
  • Long-lasting results - Permanent fat cell removal with proper maintenance

Types of 360 Degree HD Liposuction

  • Manual – Traditional cannulas manually break down fat deposits. Suitable for smaller areas requiring precise control.
  • Power-Assisted Liposuction (PAL) – Uses vibrating cannulas powered by advanced technology like Microaire systems. Ideal for larger volume fat removal treatment with reduced surgeon fatigue and improved precision.
  • Ultrasound-Assisted Liposuction (Vaser) – 360 Degree HD Liposuction using ultrasound energy provides additional benefits:
    • Enhanced skin tightening
    • More precise fat removal
    • Better definition of muscular contours
    • Reduced trauma to surrounding tissues

How is 360 Degree Liposuction performed at Ara Aesthetics?

Our 360 Degree Liposuction procedure follows a meticulous approach ensuring optimal safety and results:

Pre-procedure preparation

The patient is positioned appropriately, and comprehensive markings are made to guide the contouring process.

Anesthesia administration

General anesthesia ensures complete comfort throughout the 3-4 hour procedure.

Tumescent technique

Special tumescent fluid is injected to minimize bleeding and aid fat removal.

Back contouring (Prone position)

  • Small incisions (less than 0.5mm) are strategically placed
  • Upper back (bra rolls) and lower back (love handles) are sculpted
  • Sacral dimples are enhanced for better definition

Front contouring (Supine position)

  • Abdominal etching creates natural muscle definition
  • High-definition techniques separate rectus muscles
  • Creates six-pack or curvaceous abs based on patient preference

Closure and dressing

Incisions are closed with minimal scarring, and compression garments are applied.

How do I prepare for 360 Degree Liposuction?

Ara Aesthetics provides comprehensive pre-operative guidance for optimal results

  • Stop alcohol consumption 3-5 days before surgery
  • Discontinue blood-thinning medications as advised by your physician
  • Quit smoking at least 2 weeks prior to surgery
  • Maintain normal diet the day before, then fast as instructed
  • Arrange transportation and post-operative care assistance
  • Purchase compression garments as recommended

FAQ

What happens after 360 Degree Liposuction?

Immediate Post-operative period

  • Overnight hospital stay for monitoring
  • Compression garment application begins immediately
  • Minimal drainage from incision sites (normal for 24 hours)
  • Pain management with prescribed medications

Recovery Timeline

  • Day 1: Begin wearing compression garments continuously
  • Week 1: Light household activities permitted
  • Week 2-3: Return to work and mild aerobic activities
  • Week 4-6: Continue compression garment use
  • Month 3: Significant improvement visible
  • Month 6-12: Final results achieved

Post-operative Care

  • Compression garment wear for 4-6 weeks minimum
  • Lymphatic massage to reduce swelling
  • Follow-up appointments for progress monitoring
  • Activity modification as recommended
